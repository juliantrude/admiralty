Put unemployed pops to work before founding new colonies. A new colony costs resources and
needs time before it pays back; pops that already stand idle yield nothing, so free jobs for
them first (new buildings or districts that open jobs, or a better job mix) and only then
expand. While unemployed pops exist, a new colony is not the answer to a shortage of workers.
