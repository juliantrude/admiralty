reason: >
  Seed rule of the build plan, approved by Julian: influence above the storage cap is lost, so
  income beyond the cap is wasted.
---
Influence at the cap is waste. A full influence stockpile stops collecting the monthly income,
so the surplus is lost. When the stockpile is at its cap, spend it on something that is bought with
influence (a megastructure, an orbital ring or a starbase module), or shift the pops that
produce it to something that counts. Do not advise to raise influence income while the
stockpile is full.
