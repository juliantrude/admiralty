Judge stockpiled Unity against the cost of the next Tradition, never as an absolute number.
Unity that is saved towards the next Tradition is earmarked: do not recommend spending it on a
decree or an edict, and do not call the stockpile idle while it is still below that cost. Only
Unity above the cost of the next Tradition is free to spend.
