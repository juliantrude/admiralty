# M8 plan: Muninn

Build plan: [M8](../build-plan.md). Terms: Muninn, Campaign Workspace, Snapshot, Timeline, Play Session,
Goal, Symptom, Consulting, Coaching, Fair Play, God Mode, Unverified Claim (see [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md)).
Input: the archive of [M3.md](M3.md) (`archive/<date>.json.zst`, `workspace/archive.py`), the Mechanics Graph of
[M4.md](M4.md), the claim check of [M7.md](M7.md).

**Done when** (from the build plan): Muninn over the 8-save reference Campaign (2200 to 2298) names its epochs
correctly by the Timeline and passes the Unverified Claim check.

## Division of labour

| Code (`src/hlidskjalf/muninn/`, MCP tool `retrospective`) | Model (`skills/muninn/`) |
|---|---|
| Resolves the time frame, reads the archive, builds the series per archived date, the change between neighbouring dates (with percentages), the merged Timeline, boundary candidates and finding candidates; checks the epochs the model proposes | Chooses the epochs (from the candidates and the Timeline), names them, decides what went well and badly, says what to learn, writes it in the Player's language |
| Resolves the Symptoms of every Goal on the first and last archived date of the frame (Consulting) | Judges the state of the Goals from those numbers |

Everything the answer states as a number comes out of the `retrospective` tool result, so the Unverified Claim
check (M7) has its evidence: the tool is logged in the turn log like the others. The model never computes a
figure the claim check cannot derive (M7 allows sums and differences of two to four shown numbers and
`a / b * 100`). Percent changes `(b - a) / a * 100` and spans in years are therefore delivered by the tool, not left
to the model.

## What the archive holds, and the consequence

Probed on the vulkan reference Campaign (8 saves, 2200.01.01 to 2298.03.11 by file name):

- Every extract is the **compact document** of one in-game date, keyed by the **header date**, which can differ from
  the file name by days (`2220.04.22.sav` is archived as `2220.04.26`). Muninn speaks in header dates only.
- An extract carries only the **last 20 Timeline events** and `timeline_events_total`. The full Timeline of the Tracked
  Empire has 23 events over 95 years (6 new colonies, 4 "A Law for the Stars", two wars, ...), so the newest extract
  lacks the 3 oldest, which the older extracts still hold. Decision: Muninn **merges the Timeline slices of all
  archived extracts of the Campaign** (dedupe on date, event and `involves`), and reports `timeline_complete: false`
  with the missing count when the merge holds fewer events than `timeline_events_total` of the newest extract. No
  change to the extract schema: it would invalidate every archive.
- The Timeline is **events, not numbers** and is sparse (about one event per four years). Wars show in a Snapshot only
  while they run: the reference Campaign has a war declared on 2230.07.17, in no Snapshot at all (2220 and 2245 show
  none), and a war from 2268.09.15 visible on 2273.09.07 and over by 2284.09.29. So an epoch border can be **exact**
  (a Timeline date) or **between two archived dates** (what only the Snapshots show), and the tool says which.
- Between two archived dates the archive holds nothing. The reference gaps are 20 to 25 years early on. Muninn
  therefore reports **changes per interval** and never invents a curve between two points; the skill says so when it
  states a trend.
- Extracts carry `known_empires` with `visible` and `full` facts; Fair Play must filter exactly as `military` does.

## The time frame

`retrospective(since, until)` with in-game dates (`YYYY.MM.DD`) or the words below, resolved against the archived dates:

| Request | Frame |
|---|---|
| default / `last` | **the last Play Session**: from the second newest archived date to the newest. The archive has no notion of a Play Session; this is what its dates resolve to (one entry per save read), and the result states the dates so the Player can correct it |
| `since=D` | from the newest archived date at or before D (so the frame starts on data, never inside a gap) to the newest |
| `all` | first to last archived date, "since 2200" |
| `since=D, until=E` | both ends moved to archived dates the same way |

A frame needs **two archived dates**. With fewer the result is `{"error": ...}` saying how many are archived and that
Muninn reads what the Huginn tools archived on every save they read. Before resolving, the tool reads the newest save
in the save folder through the normal path, so the newest state is archived (`CampaignService.document` does that);
without any save on disk it works from the archive alone and says the newest archived date.
Corrupt archive files are listed in `problems`, not fatal; the frame skips them.

## The tool result (`retrospective`)

One JSON document, built by `muninn/` from archived documents, trimmed to the same 40k-character budget as
`expand_graph`:

- `frame`: `from`, `to`, `request`, `dates` (every archived date in the frame), `span_years`, `campaign`,
  `tracked_empire`, `mode`, `knowledge` (fair_play or god_mode), `problems`, `timeline_complete`.
- `series`: per archived date the values the questions need: planets, pops, unemployed, military / economy / tech power,
  victory rank, naval capacity used and max, number of Traditions and Ascension Perks, wars running, the monthly net and
  stockpile of every resource (Display Name labels as in the extract), and `known_empires` filtered by the Campaign's
  knowledge. Names come from the extract; nothing is re-localised.
- `intervals`: between every neighbouring pair `from`, `to`, `years`, and for each series value `from`, `to`, `change`,
  `change_pct` (absent when `from` is 0); planets gained and lost, Traditions and Perks added, wars started and ended,
  by `diff_extracts` (the existing function, unchanged).
- `timeline`: the merged events of the frame (`date`, `event`, `involves`) and `before_frame` (their count).
- `epoch_candidates`: boundary candidates (below).
- `findings`: finding candidates (below).
- `goals` (Consulting only): per Goal of the Mechanics Graph the Symptom metrics resolved on the first and the last date
  of the frame (`graph.expand.catalogue` on those two archived documents; the pack comes from the extract's
  `game_version`, a missing pack is an error inside `goals` only). Coaching Campaigns get no `goals` key: the Player's
  own assessment comes first there (see "Coaching" below).

## Epochs

An **epoch** is a stretch of the Campaign that one development defined (an expansion, a war, a stagnation). The code
cannot name them but must keep the model honest: it hands over where borders can lawfully be and checks the result.

**Boundary candidates** (`muninn/epochs.py`, pure, deterministic), each `{date, precision, reasons[]}`:

- every **Timeline event** is a candidate with precision `exact` and the event as its reason. The event text is the
  localised title; the code does not guess its meaning from the title (locale dependent; the extract stays as it is);
- a **war** in an interval's `wars_started` / `wars_ended` gives a candidate with precision `between` (`after` and
  `before` the two archived dates) and the war name;
- a **regime change** in a series value: the growth per year of planets, pops, military power or economy power in one
  interval differs from the previous interval by more than a factor of 2 (both nonzero), or the sign flips: reason
  `"<series> <growth>/yr -> <growth>/yr"`, precision `between`. The rule is one table at the top of the module so it can
  be tuned, and its numbers are in the result so the claim check passes them;
- the **frame ends** are always borders.

Candidates within 365 days of each other are not merged (the model decides), but are listed in date order, so the model
sees the cluster.

**The epochs the model proposes** go in a small JSON file (`epochs.json`: a list of `{title, from, to, anchor,
why}`) that `hlidskjalf muninn check-epochs --workspace DIR --epochs FILE --since/--until` validates against the same
frame and Timeline (the skill runs it, like `graph check-solution`):

- the epochs are contiguous, in date order, first `from` and last `to` are the frame ends, no overlap, at least one;
- every `from` after the first one has an `anchor` that names a candidate: a Timeline event date and its `event`, or the
  two archived dates of a `between` border. An anchor that is in no candidate is an error ("no Timeline event or
  archived interval at 2240.01.01"). This is what "names its epochs correctly by the Timeline" means in code: no border
  without a dated event or an interval the Snapshots show;
- `title` and `why` are non-empty; digits in a `title` are a warning (a figure belongs in the sentence the claim check
  sees, not in a heading);
- a gap of more than 15 years inside one epoch with a Timeline event or a candidate in it is a warning
  ("2230.07.17 War Declared lies inside the epoch ..."), because that is where epochs are usually missed.

## Findings

`findings` (`muninn/findings.py`) are candidates for "what went well / badly", **not verdicts**: per interval, the
largest relative gains and losses among the series values (by `change_pct`, with the absolute change), a stockpile that
reached zero or is at zero on the last date, a resource net that turned negative, a rank change, planets lost, a war
that cost military power (war running at one end and `military_power` down), unemployed pops that grew faster than pops.
Each is `{kind, interval, subject, from, to, change, change_pct}`, ordered by size, at most 12 per interval. The model
picks which are good or bad and why; the code does not judge. No Benchmark is used: all Benchmark values are still null
(hold point) and the skill states that it compares the Player with their own earlier state, never with other players.

## Coaching

Item 5 of M9 (self-assessment first, Player Model update) belongs to M9. M8 builds the part that exists already: in a
Coaching Campaign the skill asks the Player for their own assessment of the frame first, in one question, waits for the
answer, then gives the same analysis, without `goals` and without any Player Model write, and says nothing about
Predictions. M9 completes that branch.

## The skill (`skills/muninn/SKILL.md`, name `muninn`)

Frontmatter description as in the consult skill (when to use it: the Player asks how a session or the Campaign went,
what to learn, "Rückblick", a retrospective). Steps:

1. `campaign_status` for the age warning and stale save; relay warnings. Ask for the frame if the Player gave none
   ("last session" is the default and is stated with its dates).
2. `retrospective` for the frame. Read `problems`, `timeline_complete` and `frame`, state the dates actually used.
3. Epochs: pick from `epoch_candidates`, write `epochs.json`, run `muninn check-epochs`, fix until it is clean.
4. Per epoch: what happened (Timeline events, intervals), what went well, what went badly, from the `findings`; every
   figure from the tool result, every Display Name in **bold** (the claim check reads bold as a name).
5. What to learn: two to four lessons, each tied to a finding and where it shows. Consulting: the state of the Goals
   (`goals`), first date against last date, which Symptom moved and which did not. Playbook Rules are cited only when a
   result delivered them; the skill does not add rules (hold point).
6. Honesty: say where the archive has a gap (between two dates nothing is known), that war and Timeline events outside
   the Snapshots are exact only to the Timeline, and that no Benchmark was used.

Hard rules as in the consult skill: numbers only from tool results; no Benchmarks; Fair Play; no HTML; Display Names
exactly as returned, never a raw key; a tool error stops the step and is reported.

## Open risks

- **Sparse data.** Eight dates over 98 years, 20 to 25 years apart early on: an epoch border between two dates is a
  statement about an interval, not a day. The result says `between`, the skill repeats it; a Campaign with saves read
  every Play Session will give finer borders.
- **Timeline titles are localised** and not classified by the code. The model reads them; `check-epochs` only checks
  that the anchor exists. If the review of the first Muninn runs shows wrong readings, a `kind` per Timeline event
  (needs the definition id in the extract and a schema bump) is the proposal, not a change inside M8.
- **Last Play Session = last two archived dates** is an approximation. If the Player played twice without any tool
  call in between, one entry covers both; the stated dates let them correct it.
- **Stale archive.** Muninn is only as current as the last save any Huginn tool read; the tool reads the newest save
  first and warns when that save is older than the newest archived date.
- **Result size.** 8 dates are small (a few k characters); a Campaign with 60 saves needs the trim: the budget step
  drops `series` detail of interior dates (keeps the first, the last and every border) and says so in `warnings`.
